# Afai Pixel emotional story film: "Your dream deserves to be seen"

`afaipixel-your-dream.mp4`: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 70.5 s. A hand-built illustrated story with a warm British female voice-over, piano and strings. Upload `cover.jpg` as the cover.

## The idea
The viewer is the hero. Every small-business owner sacrifices for their dream, but online nobody sees it. Afai Pixel's green pixels build their brand and website, and the first message and first order arrive. The film ends by inviting them to send a message.
