## Suggested campaign setup (Meta Ads Manager)
- **Objective:** Engagement → Messages (Messenger and WhatsApp), or Leads with an instant form (name, email, phone, "What kind of website do you need?").
- **Budget:** run one ad set at a steady daily budget for at least 5–7 days so Meta can learn; spend more on Black Friday weekend itself (27–30 November).
- **Audience:** small-business owners and decision makers. Interests such as Small business, Entrepreneurship, Shopify, WooCommerce, E-commerce, Digital marketing, Business owner (behaviour: "Page admins"). Ages 24–60.
- **Locations:** Sri Lanka, the UK and other markets you serve, as separate ad sets if budgets differ, so each currency and time zone is optimised on its own.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements, with the 4:5 files for feeds and the 9:16 files for Reels and Stories.
- **Testing:** start with the video (4:5 + 9:16) and the image (4:5 + 9:16) in the same ad set, with copy A and B. Keep the winner after 3–4 days.
- **Retargeting:** a second small ad set for people who watched 50% of the video or visited afaipixel.com, using copy C and the story image.
